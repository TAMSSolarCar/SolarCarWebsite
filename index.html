Our Mission

Our solar car team is focused on preparing for the National Solar Car Challenge. Through innovation, collaboration, and renewable energy engineering, we are building a reliable, high-performance race vehicle.

This project gives students real engineering experience under competitive constraints. Every design decision, test session, and iteration moves us closer to race day while advancing practical solar technology and student innovation.
